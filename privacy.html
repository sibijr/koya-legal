Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026. Last updated 28 September 2026.

Koya writes you short stories about your own life, set tomorrow and written as though they already happened, and reads them back in a voice you recorded yourself. To do that it has to know things about you that are genuinely personal: what you want, what is hard right now, who matters to you, and what your voice sounds like.

This policy says exactly what we hold, where it lives, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it. It describes what the app actually does today, not what we might do later.

The short version.

  • Your answers, your stories and your voice are yours. We do not sell them and we do not use them to advertise to you.
  • Our analytics carry counts and durations, never the content of your stories or answers.
  • Your data is stored in India. Analytics are processed in the United States.
  • Deleting your account deletes everything, including the voice model held at our speech provider.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

1. Who we are

Koya is operated by the developer of the Koya iOS app ("Koya", "we", "us"), who is the data controller for the information described here. For any privacy question, including a request to access or delete your data, contact us at koyamanifest@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account

Koya uses Sign in with Apple. We receive a stable identifier for your Apple Account and, if you allow it, an email address. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, what we receive is Apple's private relay address rather than your real one, and we never learn your real address. We do not operate passwords, so there is no Koya password to lose.

What you tell us during onboarding and afterwards

  • Your first name, and optionally your city, age, gender and occupation.
  • The language you want Koya to speak, your locale and your time zone.
  • Free-text answers about what you want and what is hard for you at the moment.
  • The desires you are working towards, and the people who matter to you, including the names and relationships you choose to give us.
  • Topics you never want mentioned, and corrections you make to how Koya writes about you.

This is the personalisation material. It is the reason a Koya story is about you rather than about nobody. Some of what you write here may reveal things that count as sensitive in your country, such as health, beliefs or relationships. We do not ask for that, we do not use it to categorise you, and we hold it only to write your stories.

Voice

  • The audio you record so Koya can read in your own voice, and the voice model built from it.
  • Short clips you record to teach Koya how to pronounce a particular name or word.
  • Audio you dictate instead of typing, which is converted to text.
  • A record that you consented to voice cloning: the version of the consent text, the time you accepted it, and a one-way hash of the IP address it came from. We keep the hash, not the address, because the purpose is to evidence that consent happened and nothing more.

What Koya makes for you

The stories, affirmations, narration and artwork generated for your account, plus which of them you played, how far you got, and when.

Sleep audio is not personalised. The sleep tracks in Koya are a shared library that is the same for everybody. They are not written from your answers, not generated for you, and contain nothing about you. We record that you played one, in the same way we record any other playback.

Subscription

Whether your subscription is active, which product it is, and when it expires. Payment itself is handled by Apple. We never see your card, and we never receive your payment details.

Device and technical data

Collected automatically when you use the app: a device and installation identifier, your device model and operating system version, the Koya app version, your approximate country inferred from your IP address, and the IP address itself as part of ordinary network traffic. We do not collect precise location, and Koya never asks for location permission.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store the times you want to be reminded. Koya's daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device rather than pushed from our servers, so the reminder itself does not send anything about you to us.

4. What our analytics never contain

Koya's product analytics carry identifiers, counts, durations, languages and outcomes. They never carry the content of your life.

No story title, no story text, no affirmation, no desire, no name of a person you told us about, no answer you typed, and no audio. We record that a story was played and for how long. We do not record what the story said.

This is enforced in a single file in the app that declares every event Koya can send, so it is a property of the code rather than a promise about our intentions. Analytics are attached to your account identifier so we can answer questions like how many people finish a story, and they are cleared when you sign out. Session recording and automatic screen capture are switched off.

6. Where your data lives, and when it crosses a border

Your account, your answers, your voice recordings and everything Koya generates for you are stored in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai, India) region.

Two flows leave that region. Our AI providers process content on their own infrastructure, which may be outside India, in order to write your stories and synthesise speech. Our analytics provider processes the events in section 4 in the United States. Where those transfers involve personal data of people in the UK or the EEA, they are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent transfer mechanism offered by the processor. By using Koya from outside India you understand that your information will be processed as described here.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, we keep your profile, your personalisation material, your voice recordings and model, and everything Koya has written for you, because the product is the accumulation of it.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, there is a seven-day grace period during which you can change your mind by signing back in. After seven days the deletion runs automatically and cannot be undone.
  • Records we must keep, such as transaction records required for tax or accounting, are retained for as long as the law requires, separately from your account.

Separately, if you do not open Koya for three days we stop generating new stories for you. That is a cost control, not a deletion: nothing of yours is removed, and it resumes when you come back.

8. Deleting your account, and what that actually does

You can delete your account from Settings in the app. Deletion runs in a deliberate order:

  • First, your voice models are deleted at our speech provider. If that step fails, the whole deletion stops and is retried. We would rather fail loudly than leave a clone of your voice at a third party after you asked to be forgotten.
  • Then the raw voice recordings are deleted.
  • Then the generated narration and artwork are deleted.
  • Finally your account itself is deleted, which removes every remaining row about you.

Your voice is deleted with your account, and only with your account. Koya does not currently offer a way to delete a recorded voice while keeping the rest of your account. If you want your voice removed, delete your account, or write to us and we will remove it for you.

Deleting your Koya account does not cancel your Apple subscription. Apple subscriptions are managed by Apple, and you cancel them in your App Store account settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, withdraw a consent you gave, receive a copy in a portable form, and complain to your data protection authority.

Several of these are built into the app: you can edit your profile and desires, remove tags and topics Koya has inferred, and delete your account. For anything else, write to koyamanifest@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you for exercising a right, and we will not treat you differently for having exercised one.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened lawfully before you withdrew it.

10. United States residents

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Virginia or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to know what we collect and why, to access and delete it, to correct it, to obtain a portable copy, to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. There is therefore nothing to opt out of, but you may still make any of the other requests above by writing to us.

If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. We will respond to an appeal in writing. California residents may also request details of any disclosure of personal information to third parties for direct marketing under California's "Shine The Light" law; we make no such disclosures.

11. Other regions

If you are in the UK or EEA, you may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the UK Information Commissioner's Office. If you are in Switzerland, you may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

If you are in Australia, we handle personal information consistently with the Privacy Act 1988 and you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. In New Zealand, the Privacy Act 2020 applies and you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. In South Africa, you may complain to the Information Regulator under POPIA. In Canada, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In India, you may contact us using the details in section 17.

12. Automated processing

Koya uses AI to decide what to write for you and which artwork to show. That is automated processing, and it shapes your experience of the product. It does not produce legal effects and it does not significantly affect you in the sense those laws mean: it decides what a story says, not whether you get credit, a job, insurance or a service.

We do not use automated processing to profile you for advertising. You can correct what Koya believes about you at any time, and tell it what never to mention.

13. Do Not Track

Most browsers and some operating systems offer a Do Not Track signal. There is no agreed standard for responding to it, so Koya does not respond to Do Not Track signals. If that changes we will say so here. Koya does not track you across other companies' apps or websites in any case.

14. Children

Koya is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use it. It is not intended for, and not directed at, anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and the app will not accept an age below it.

We ask for 18 rather than a lower age because Koya records your voice and builds a biometric model of it, and because it asks what is difficult in your life. Neither is something a minor can meaningfully consent to. If you believe someone under 18 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted per user by row-level security, so one account cannot read another's rows. Voice recordings and generated audio are held in private storage that is not publicly listable. Access to our production systems is limited to the people who operate Koya.

No system is perfectly secure, and we are not going to claim otherwise. We do not hold your payment details or a password for you, which removes the two things most worth stealing.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

17. Contact, and how to review or delete your data

To review or correct what we hold, open Settings in the app, where you can edit your profile, your desires, the people you told us about, and the topics Koya has inferred. To delete everything, use Delete account in Settings.

For anything else, including a formal access or deletion request: koyamanifest@gmail.com
