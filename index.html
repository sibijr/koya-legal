Written as though it already happened

Not a generic affirmation. Your actual life, tomorrow.

Koya knows the job you are chasing, the city you love, the people you would call first. It writes in the past tense, as a memory of a day that went the way you hoped.

Three new stories every morning. About two minutes each.

How Koya works

Three steps. About five minutes.

1

Tell it what you want

Your goals, what is hard right now, the people who matter. Correct it any time and tomorrow's story changes.

2

Record your voice once

Thirty seconds is enough. Koya builds a reading voice from it, privately, just for you.

3

Wake up to your future

New stories every morning, written as memories of days that already went your way.

Why your own voice

The voice you trust most is already yours.

Hearing your future in a stranger's voice is a podcast. Hearing it in your own voice feels like a memory. That is the whole idea behind Koya.
