Support

Write to us at koyamanifest@gmail.com and a person will reply, usually within two working days.

Something broken, or a story that landed badly? Tell us what happened and what you expected instead. If it is about a particular story, the day it appeared is enough. We do not need you to quote it back, and you should not have to.

Subscriptions and billing

How do I cancel?

In the store that billed you, not in Koya. Only that store can end the subscription.

  • iPhone: open App Store subscription settings, choose Koya, then Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play subscriptions, choose Koya, then Cancel subscription.

Deleting your Koya account does not cancel your subscription. They are two separate things and you have to do both.

I cancelled. Why do I still have access?

Because you paid for the period you are in. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not end the time you already bought. Access stops at the end of the current period.

Can I have a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us, and we have no way to issue one. For the App Store, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, follow Google's refund steps.

Koya says my free sample is used up, but I never used it

On iPhone, the free sample is one per device, not one per account, so making a new account on the same phone does not bring it back. If you bought this phone second hand, the person who had it before you may have used it. That is not your fault and we can reset it. Write to us from the phone in question and say so.

I paid and Koya still says I need a subscription

Give it a minute and reopen the app. Koya confirms a purchase with its own server rather than taking the app's word for it, and occasionally that confirmation arrives a little after the store's does. If it has not cleared after a few minutes, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still has not, write to us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it.

I have a new phone

Sign in the same way as before (the same Apple Account on iPhone, the same Google Account on Android), then open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Everything written for you is on our servers, not on the old phone, so nothing is lost.

Your voice

How do I get Koya to read in my voice?

Tap My voice at the top of the home screen and record the passage it gives you somewhere quiet. It takes a couple of minutes. Your stories are read in that voice from then on.

Can I delete my voice without deleting my account?

Not from inside the app yet. Today a recorded voice is removed when the account is deleted. If you want your voice removed on its own, write to us and we will delete it, including the model held at our speech provider.

It does not sound like me

Usually the recording. Background noise, a fan, or a room with a lot of echo all end up in the model. Re-record somewhere quiet, close to the phone, at your ordinary speaking pace.

It says a name wrong

Teach it. In Settings, under how Koya sounds, you can record how a particular name or word should be said, and it will use your pronunciation from then on.

The stories

Where are today's stories?

They are written overnight and are usually waiting when you wake. If the rail is empty, pull it down to refresh. If Koya has not heard from you in three days it stops writing new ones to avoid spending on somebody who has stopped listening; opening the app starts it again.

Koya keeps writing about something I do not want

Open the You tab and add it under Never mentions. It will not come up again. You can also correct anything Koya believes about you in the same place, and the correction shapes what gets written tomorrow.

A story got something wrong about me

Tell it. The box at the top of the You tab takes a plain sentence, and that sentence changes what is written next. You do not have to re-do onboarding.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

In Settings, choose Delete account, then type delete my account to confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. It removes your voice model at our speech provider first, then the recordings, then everything written for you, then the account itself, and signs you out.

What do you collect, and who else sees it?

Set out plainly in the Privacy Policy, including the list of processors and what each one receives. The short version: your stories and answers are not in our analytics, and we do not sell anything.

Still stuck

Write to koyamanifest@gmail.com. Telling us your device, your iOS or Android version and the Koya version from the bottom of the Settings screen will usually save a round trip.
